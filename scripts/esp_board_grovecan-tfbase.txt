The hardware this image is for
------------------------------
M5 ATOM Lite with an M5 Unit CAN plugged into the Grove port and an Atomic TF
Card Base fitted under it. The CAN pins are GPIO 26 (TX, the Grove cable's
yellow wire) and GPIO 32 (RX, the white wire). The card's SPI pins are GPIO 19
(MOSI), GPIO 33 (MISO) and GPIO 23 (CLK); the base has no chip select line.

With a card of 16 GB or less in the base, the monitor page gets an SD card
screen: card status, eject and format, a file browser with download, upload and
delete, and a recording of the CAN bus to the card that can start by itself at
power-up. On one scooter's bus it recorded every frame to the card without losing
one, including with the ECU's debug frames on (about 585 frames a second).

Flash this image only on that hardware. On an ATOM Lite with the Atomic CAN
Base it would listen on the wrong pins and see nothing; that hardware has its
own package (the one with "canbase" in its name). Every image prints the
hardware it was built for in the boot log ("Hardware: ...").

A loose 120 ohm resistor comes in the Unit CAN's box. Leave it out on the
scooter: the scooter's bus is already terminated.

These pins have been tried on one scooter's bus, where receiving and sending
both worked. If the monitor shows no frames with the Unit CAN connected, the
CAN pins are the first thing to check.
