The hardware this image is for
------------------------------
M5 ATOM Lite with the Atomic CAN Base. The CAN pins are GPIO 22 (TX) and
GPIO 19 (RX).

Flash this image only on that hardware. On an ATOM Lite with a Unit CAN on the
Grove port it would listen on the wrong pins and see nothing; that hardware has
its own package (the one with "grovecan" in its name). Every image prints the
hardware it was built for in the boot log ("Hardware: ...").
